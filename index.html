About Us

Timveka is a product of Adeptus Software Solutions (PACRA Business Registration No. 320261079900) .

Mission Statement:
To brigde the gap between delivery of services from artist/producer to client and the ensuring that producers and artists get their appropriate and rightful compensation.

This service is designed to enable every producer/artist around the world not to only market their respective catalogues, but also to enable producers to sell custom beat production services without having to worry about getting paid. All this while simultaneously safe guarding the interest of their clients and preserving their right to be reimbursed should anything go wrong in the course of provision of the afore mentioned services.


Our Core Values:
We at Adeptus Software Solutions, believe in providing commerical solutions that guarantee the online safety of all stakeholders affected both directly and indirectly through the use of our products and services, while protecting their resources. We ensure that all procedures involved in the operations of our services are straight forward, transparent and easy to understand, thus creating a suitable climate for verifiability and accountability.

Our Promise:
To all our stakeholders and their customers, our pledge is uncompromised safety, security and quality. By using this service, the success of your production business is secure.

Founder and Head Developer
